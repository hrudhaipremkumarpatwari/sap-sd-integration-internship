# Week 1 — Strategic Planning for SAP SD Integration

## Task outcome

Prepare a professional DOCX strategic plan aligning business needs and technical feasibility for a proposed SAP SD integration. The supplied brief expects requirements, a detailed timeline with milestones and contingency, a budget estimate, risk analysis, and resource allocation.

## Recommended report contents

- Executive summary, case context, objectives, scope, and success criteria.
- Business and technical requirements, priorities, dependencies, and acceptance measures.
- Options considered and a reasoned feasibility recommendation.
- Phased plan with milestones, deliverables, dependencies, owners, and schedule contingency.
- Estimate basis for budget and effort; separate assumed rates and quantities from sourced facts.
- Roles and responsibilities with estimated hours; identify where estimates are illustrative.
- Risk register with probability/impact scale, owner, mitigation, trigger, contingency, and residual risk.
- Governance, decision points, assumptions, constraints, and references.

## Evidence boundary

The task brief asks for approximately 30–35 hours of work. This is expected effort, not a completed-hours claim. Do not imply organizational requirements, approved budget, or stakeholder consultation unless evidenced.

- `report/`: final DOCX and optional PDF preview.
- `supporting/`: requirements matrix, schedule, budget assumptions, and risk register source files.
